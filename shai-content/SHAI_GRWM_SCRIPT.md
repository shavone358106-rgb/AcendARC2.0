## Edit plan
- **Pacing:** cut every 2–4 s. Jump-cut out every breath and pause. First cut lands by 1.5 s.
- **Captions:** burned in, word by word, white with a soft black shadow, centred in the lower third. Keep them out of the bottom 15% (app UI sits there).
- **Sound:** a soft lo-fi or neo-soul bed at −18 dB under the VO. ASMR hits on the brush taps, gloss click and compact snap. Add a faint London street ambience at 32 s.
- **Transitions:** the match cut at 48 s (gloss swipe → rooftop) is the one flourish. Everything else is a hard cut.
- **Colour:** warm, true-to-skin grade. Don't smooth the skin. The texture is what makes the face read as real.
- **Disclosure:** turn on the platform's **AI-generated content** label (TikTok and Instagram both require it for realistic AI people) and add `#AIcreator` / `#digitalcreator` to the caption.

# STYLE.md — my locked look

Four words: **simple, bold, big, clean.** Every frame reads in about one second for a stranger.
Premium comes from light, depth and smooth motion — never from adding more stuff.
If a polish pass makes a frame slower to read, undo it. Change the look on purpose, once, after a side-by-side test.

(The machine-readable copy of these colours/fonts is `style.json`. Edit both together, or run Prompt 3.)

## Two tones
| | Dark (home) | Light (paper) |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | #14100d warm black, soft radial glow, faint dot grid, light grain | #f4e9dc paper |
| Text | #f6ece2 | #2a1710 |
| Accent (one only) | #ff7a3d orange, glows | #e2551b |

- Hook and ending are always dark. Designed graphics split roughly 40–60% dark/light.
- Flip tone in runs, on a hard cut, when the idea changes. Never fade between tones. Never flip every beat.
- Real screenshots keep their own tone.

## Type
- Titles / numbers / captions: Inter Black (fallback: any heavy sans). Body: Inter Medium. Labels: mono.
- Frame-0 title: 3–8 words, leads with what the viewer gets, poses the question the short answers. It becomes the cover.

## Layout (home)
Graphic in the top two thirds, one caption line, face as a cutout in a small rounded card at the bottom. Centre everything.
Safe zones: no text in the top strip, the bottom fifth, or the right edge of the lower half.

## Captions
1–2 words at a time, cut on the spoken word, big and bold, never on top of a graphic, hidden when the graphic already shows the words.

## Motion
Numbers always count up. Lists add one row per spoken item. Something new lands about every second.
A whoosh on a cut, a tick on a build step, one energetic music bed well below the voice.

## Never do
1. Never more than one accent colour.
2. Never text in the safe zones.
3. Never a graphic hugging one side with empty space on the other.
4. Never a screenshot that can't be read in one second at phone size — build our own graphic with the real number.
5. Never redraw a logo — use the real one.

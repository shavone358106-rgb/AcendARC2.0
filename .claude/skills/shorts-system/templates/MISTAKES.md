# Mistakes we fixed (never repeat these)

Add one line every time a short needed a fix. The skill reads this before every build.

- Spoken money must be words ("five dollars and seventy cents"); captions/graphics keep numerals.
- Never pick the hook by gut: winner must be >= 3x its creator's median views, keep >= 70% of its words.
- Never cut during the hook. Anything under 0.5s is a build step, not a new shot.
- Don't paste the Instagram caption on TikTok/YouTube/X/LinkedIn (no "comment WORD" there).
- Check every link right before posting; skip the slot if one fails.

# Before you post — the checklist

**Hook and script**
- [ ] One topic, one short, one lane. The CTA line matches the lane, word for word.
- [ ] The hook comes from a winner at 3x or more, word for word, only the slots swapped.
- [ ] A stranger gets every line on the first listen. Every number has a source.
- [ ] Money is written as words for the voice. The script was locked before the voice was made.

**The picture**
- [ ] The hook holds with no cuts until the first sentence ends.
- [ ] Every claim shows the real thing within 2 seconds. Every screenshot reads in one second, or it became our own graphic.
- [ ] Nothing readable sits in the top strip, the bottom fifth or the right-edge buttons.
- [ ] Captions are 1 or 2 words and land on the spoken word.
- [ ] You watched it on your phone, with sound, start to finish.

**The loop**
- [ ] The keyword passed the check and no other automation uses it.
- [ ] You tested the DM from a second account, follow gate included.
- [ ] Instagram and Facebook captions open with the comment line. The others carry the link.
- [ ] The cover still works after the profile grid crops it (`cover_gridcrop.jpg`).
- [ ] You said go. Nothing was posted by the skill alone.

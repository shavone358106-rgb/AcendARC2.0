# Script rules (stages 3–4: hook + script lock)

A short has one job: get the right person to raise their hand. Real payoff in ~30 seconds, one question left open, one ask.

## Steal the hook, don't write it
- Start from someone else's winning opening, word for word. Only swap the slots: the tool, the topic, the number, the audience.
- Only real winners: the source video got at least 3x its creator's median views. Under that, stop and pick another.
- Keep at least 70% of the original words, in the original order. If it needs more change, pick a different winner.
- It must read cold: no inside terms, no jargon, no "it"/"this" with nothing to point at.
- Never invent a number, never round one up. Write the source link + views next to the hook.

## Script shape (105–125 words)
1. **Hook (line 1):** the stolen hook, slots swapped.
2. **Problem (line 2):** the viewer's real problem in plain words.
3. **Steps:** say the order out loud — "First…" then "Then…".
4. **Twist:** "But here's the trick if you want to [bigger outcome]. Don't [obvious move]. Instead, [better move]."
5. **List of 3:** three things a graphic can show. "But here's the best part" goes before the best one, not the first.
6. **Pre-CTA + CTA:** what only the link gives you, then the lane's CTA line word for word.

## Checks (the cold read)
- Every line clear on its own to a stranger; offer a plainer line when not.
- Every line ends on a noun or number a graphic can show.
- No hype word without a number next to it.
- Money written as words for the voice ("five dollars and seventy cents"). Numerals only in captions and graphics.
- Every number has a source the creator gave. Cut any that don't.
- Once the creator says "locked", don't change a word (every change after the voice costs a new render).

## Lanes and CTA lines
| Lane | Source | CTA line | They get |
|---|---|---|---|
| top (~60%) | other creators' outliers, repos taking off, launches | If you want [the thing], comment [WORD]. | free guide page |
| middle (~20%) | your long videos not yet cut | If you want the full video, comment [WORD]. | the full video |
| bottom (~20%) | one lesson from your paid community | The full lesson is inside [community]. Comment CLUB. | the community (fixed word) |
| ad (extension) | your product / offer | If you want [the offer], comment [WORD]. | offer page / booking link |

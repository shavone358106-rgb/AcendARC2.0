# Magazine Cover

**What it looks like:** The frame turns into a magazine cover: a huge masthead across the top **behind** the speaker's head, cover lines on both sides, issue date, a barcode in the corner, and a round sticker ('20 effects inside'). The speaker stays live in front of it.

**When to use it:** Personal brand moments, announcements, 'featured' or authority segments.

**Timing:** Masthead slides down 0.3 s, cover lines stagger in 80 ms apart, the sticker spins in last with an overshoot.

**Brand hooks:** Masthead in `accent_color` using `font` at heavy weight; background off-white or `primary_color`. Masthead text is the owner's brand name.

## How to build it

- Speaker matte over a cover layout; the masthead must sit between the background and the matte so the head overlaps it, like a real cover.
- Barcode: an SVG of random bars, never a real product's code.
- Cover lines come from the transcript.

## Check before shipping

- Reads on a phone at full speed; stays inside the 9:16 safe zone.
- Doesn't hide the speaker's face or the captions for longer than the beat needs.
